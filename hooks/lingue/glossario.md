English is the source.

Some terms stay in English in every language, as in the host app: effort, worktree, fork, token, Claude, Codex,
FreeLLMAPI, DeepSeek and board.

## Rules

- Buttons are short imperatives: «Start», «Avvia», «Iniciar», «Lancer», «Starten», «Iniciar».
- Sentences, not title case. No ALL CAPS, no emoji.
- Placeholders such as `{n}` and `{titolo}` stay exactly as they are in English. Signs such as ● ○ ▲ ✓ stay too.
- German and French run long. Keep labels within about 1.4× the English length; the 60-column test checks it.
- The other languages are machine-translated. Corrections are welcome.
